Care AVL

Technology Designed Around People and Care

Explore Solutions → How We Compare →

Care AVL specializes in audio, video, and lighting (AVL) systems—including the communication and collaboration technology—for healthcare environments.

Healthcare facilities aren't ordinary commercial spaces. Technology must function around clinicians, patients, administrators, educators and families—often in environments where communication, reliability and ease of use are especially important.

Care AVL brings together healthcare-focused system designers, industry-trained technical professionals and specialized installation teams who understand the demands of healthcare environments.

Better technology should support better communication—not complicate it.

Technology Your People Know How to Use.

Our work doesn't end with installation.

Care AVL guides organizations through discovery, system design, installation, programming, commissioning and training—always considering how the technology will actually be used once we're gone.

We train the teams who will operate these environments so they understand not only which buttons to press, but how to use the technology to support communication, education, collaboration and the experience their organization wants to provide.

The Running Tuba Process

How We Compare

Use this to evaluate any provider—including us. The goal is a fair comparison of total capability and value, not the lowest number on a page.

Evaluation Area Our Standard Questions to Ask Any Provider
Industry-Specific Discovery Discovery is led by specialists who work in healthcare environments every day and start with how the space is actually used. Who will lead discovery, and how many projects like mine have they personally worked on?
Relevant Design Expertise Systems are designed around the workflows, sightlines, acoustics and operators specific to healthcare environments. Will the design be tailored to my environment, or adapted from a standard room template?
Specialized Installation Experience Installation teams are experienced in healthcare environments and the access, scheduling and safety realities that come with them. Who is physically performing the installation, and are they employees or subcontracted labor?
Scope Completeness Proposals identify infrastructure, programming, integration and training up front—not as later change orders. What is explicitly excluded from this quote, and what would trigger a change order?
Commissioning & Testing Every system is tuned, tested and validated against the design intent before handover. What does your commissioning process include, and will I receive the test results?
User Training Training is built into the project for the people who will operate the room—not a rushed handoff on the last day. How much training is included, who delivers it, and what happens when staff turn over?
Documentation As-built drawings, configuration files, credentials and operating guides are delivered as part of closeout. What documentation do I receive at closeout, and do I own the configuration files?
Manufacturer Training & Certifications Technical staff maintain current manufacturer training on the platforms we design and deploy. Which manufacturers is your team currently trained on for the products you are proposing?
Service & Support Support is structured around response expectations, escalation paths and a team that already knows your system. What are your response times, and who answers when something fails during a live event?
Lifecycle Value Design decisions account for serviceability, upgrade paths and the useful life of the platform. How long will this platform be supported, and what does the upgrade path look like in five years?

This comparison describes how Running Tuba approaches projects and offers neutral questions worth asking any provider. It makes no claims about any specific competitor.
