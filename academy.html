Academy AVL

Technology Designed for the Way Education Happens

Explore Solutions → How We Compare →

Academy AVL is dedicated to the audio, video, and lighting (AVL) needs of K-12 schools, colleges and universities—along with the communication and collaboration systems that connect a campus.

Education technology isn't simply commercial equipment installed in a school. Classrooms, auditoriums, athletic facilities, performing arts centers and campus-wide systems each place different demands on technology—and on the people designing and installing it.

That's why Academy AVL brings together education-focused system designers, industry-trained technical professionals and specialized installation teams who understand how technology must perform in real learning environments.

Technology is only successful when the people it was designed for can use it successfully.

We Don't Just Install It. We Help You Use It.

Our responsibility doesn't end when the system powers on.

From discovery and system design through installation, programming, commissioning and training, Academy AVL works alongside your team to ensure the technology supports the experience you set out to create.

We train the educators, administrators and technology teams who will actually use the systems—helping turn sophisticated technology into something people feel confident using every day.

The Running Tuba Process

How We Compare

Use this to evaluate any provider—including us. The goal is a fair comparison of total capability and value, not the lowest number on a page.

Evaluation Area Our Standard Questions to Ask Any Provider
Industry-Specific Discovery Discovery is led by specialists who work in learning environments every day and start with how the space is actually used. Who will lead discovery, and how many projects like mine have they personally worked on?
Relevant Design Expertise Systems are designed around the workflows, sightlines, acoustics and operators specific to learning environments. Will the design be tailored to my environment, or adapted from a standard room template?
Specialized Installation Experience Installation teams are experienced in learning environments and the access, scheduling and safety realities that come with them. Who is physically performing the installation, and are they employees or subcontracted labor?
Scope Completeness Proposals identify infrastructure, programming, integration and training up front—not as later change orders. What is explicitly excluded from this quote, and what would trigger a change order?
Commissioning & Testing Every system is tuned, tested and validated against the design intent before handover. What does your commissioning process include, and will I receive the test results?
User Training Training is built into the project for the people who will operate the room—not a rushed handoff on the last day. How much training is included, who delivers it, and what happens when staff turn over?
Documentation As-built drawings, configuration files, credentials and operating guides are delivered as part of closeout. What documentation do I receive at closeout, and do I own the configuration files?
Manufacturer Training & Certifications Technical staff maintain current manufacturer training on the platforms we design and deploy. Which manufacturers is your team currently trained on for the products you are proposing?
Service & Support Support is structured around response expectations, escalation paths and a team that already knows your system. What are your response times, and who answers when something fails during a live event?
Lifecycle Value Design decisions account for serviceability, upgrade paths and the useful life of the platform. How long will this platform be supported, and what does the upgrade path look like in five years?

This comparison describes how Running Tuba approaches projects and offers neutral questions worth asking any provider. It makes no claims about any specific competitor.
